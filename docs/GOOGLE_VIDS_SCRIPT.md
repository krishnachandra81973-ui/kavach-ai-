# Kavach AI (कवच एआई) — Google Vids Production Prompt & Storyboard Script

> **How to use in Google Vids (vids.google.com):**
> 1. Open [Google Vids](https://vids.google.com/) on your browser.
> 2. Click **"Help me create a video"** (Gemini icon).
> 3. Paste the **Master Prompt** below into the box.
> 4. Google Vids will automatically generate the 5 scenes, choose background music, and setup the voiceover!
> 5. You can copy-paste the exact Hindi/English voiceover script for each scene as specified below.

---

## 🎬 1. Master Prompt for Google Vids (AI Prompt)

```text
Create a high-impact, professional 60-second explainer video for "Kavach AI" (कवच एआई) — a cybercrime and digital arrest defense platform built for Indian citizens. 

Style & Tone: Serious, authoritative yet reassuring and empathetic.
Visual Aesthetic: Modern cyber defense, authentic Indian contexts, mobile WhatsApp UI mockups, and police/legal iconography.
Target Audience: Indian citizens, parents, senior citizens, and startup hackathon judges.
Pacing: Fast-paced opening (hooking the viewer with the ₹1,750 Cr cybercrime problem), transitioning into a calm, reassuring demonstration of instant WhatsApp/Web verification under Indian law (BNSS 2023), and ending with a clear Call to Action (Helpline 1930 & live web platform).
```

## 💡 Quick Tips for Google Vids Customization
1. **Voiceover Option in Google Vids:** Select the *"Warm & Confident"* or *"Empathetic Storyteller"* AI voice in Google Vids settings.
2. **Music:** Choose the *"Tech Documentary"* or *"Inspiring Corporate / Hopeful"* background track at 20% volume behind the voice.
3. **Bilingual:** You can generate one video in Hindi and duplicate the project in Google Vids to swap the voiceover with the English script.
